Vigilo Confido

March 2020 – February 2025 · OpenXcom Extended (OXCE) mod · rulesets & Yankes scripts

Completion ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▱ 90% · feature complete, winnable, lightly tested

⬇ DownloadSource on GitHub

Vigilo Confido is a big expansion for OpenXcom Extended that brings ideas from the modern XCOM games back into the 1994 original. It began as a few experiments with Yankes scripts to see which skills from the remakes I could rebuild, and it got out of hand pretty quickly.

At its core is a class and skill system: Rangers, Grenadiers, Specialists, Snipers and Psi Operatives, with around 50 scripted abilities unlocked over three training phases as your soldiers earn combat decorations. On top of that come eight new aliens like the Thin Man, Sectopod Drone and Andromedon, swords, shotguns and sniper rifles in three tiers, and new art for every T2 and T3 weapon and armour.

I also rebalanced nearly everything: lasers need recharging and can blow up in the field, explosives are limited, tanks are swapped for a Gremlin drone, and you bring alien weapons home to research them. It is feature complete and playable, but mostly tested by me. Download the repo, drop it into your OXCE mods folder and enable it.

Features

  • Five soldier classes (Ranger, Grenadier, Sniper, Specialist, Psi Operative) with active and passive skills over three training phases, gated by "Combat Experience" decorations (4 kills/8 missions, then 10 kills/16 missions).
  • Around 50 scripted skills, from Run & Gun and Slash to Double Tap, Rupture, Soul Steal and Mind Control, all in Ruleset/classSkills.rul.
  • A story arc with a "Mission Objectives" Ufopedia entry that walks you from your first autopsy through the Skulljack, the Outsider shard and the Overseer UFO to the Ethereal Device.
  • Eight new aliens: Cyber Floater, Thin Man, Berserker, Armored Muton, Sectopod Drone, Muton Elite, Andromedon, Outsider.
  • Swords, shotguns and sniper rifles in three tiers (Kevlar/conventional, Predator/laser, Warden/plasma).
  • Laser weapons that recharge after a mission and can implode in the field; limited explosives.
  • The Gremlin drone instead of tanks, training rooms, status effects and hit/day-night indicators.
  • Chryssalid poison that zombifies over time, and civilians who turn out not to be civilians.
  • 500+ callsigns per class and unit response sounds; lots of extra maps from the C.M.P. pack.

Under the hood

≈ 21,000 lines of OXCE ruleset YAML & Yankes script · 536 lines of Python tools · 123 commits

The laser recharge is a returnFromMissionUnit script that simply refills whatever laser you carried home, by gun type (Ruleset/laserPlasma.rul):

if eq temp 1;
  debug_log "Recharged Laser Pistol for Recovery" item;
  item.setAmmoQuantity 12;
else eq temp 2;
  debug_log "Recharged Laser Rifle for Recovery" item;
  item.setAmmoQuantity 20;

The "civilian surprise" units count down 1-3 turns, then kill themselves on your turn so their spawnUnit: STR_FLOATER pops out (Ruleset/civilianSurprise.rul):

if eq countdown 0;
  # wake up
  unit.setHealth 0;      # kill the civilian
  debug_log "SURPRISE!";

The top of Ruleset/classSkills.rul doubles as the design doc, with a strengths/weaknesses table for every class:

# Ranger        - str: Movement/Melee,      wkn: Armor, Attack Range
# Grenadier     - str: Armor/Heavy Weapons, wkn: Movement, Accuracy
# Sniper        - str: Damage/Range,        wkn: Health, Melee
# Specialist    - str: Medikits/Support,    wkn: Offense
# Psi Op        - str: Psi,                 wkn: Health, Conventional Weapons

Balancing happened in a spreadsheet: tools/rul2csv.py and csv2rul.py round-trip the rulesets through data.sheet.ods, and graphfromrules.py draws the research tree with graphviz (there is also a TechTree.drawio).

Ideas & inspirations

The readme is upfront about it: "mechanical influences from the remakes (EU2012/XCOM2 and Phoenix Point)". Dr. Shen and Dr. Vahlen greet you in the intro, the Skulljack and Skeleton Key are straight from XCOM 2012, and the Gollop Chamber is a nod to the original's creator. A commit in February 2020 "renamed classes to xcom 2 counterparts". Graphics lean heavily on XOps' Xeno Operations, which the readme credits for sharing "the same visual vision". The goal was to add only art that keeps the X-Com look, with each tier visually distinct. The 20+ cutscenes from the remakes were later pulled for licensing reasons.

Where it started, where it ended

The first commit (January 2020) was a single 1,852-line class_skill.rul plus a psi amp script and an Area 51 integration, so it started as a skill add-on for another mod. Within two months it had become its own full overhaul with versions 0.9.x shipping almost daily in March 2020. In November 2021 I did a cleanup "making sure the mod is winnable". After that, other people kept it alive: Buscher fixed it for OXCE 7.1 and 8.0, and efrenespartano did a "big update, big overhaul" in 2023.

State of the repo

Last activity is February 2025, a merged pull request that removes ruleset entries the engine no longer knows and adds mimic soldier names for OXCE 8.1. It targets OXCE 8.0. A few TODOs remain: a "debug: TODO change back" storage value in facilities.rul, placeholder melee sounds, healing chryssalid poison, and a Ufopedia article for the laser recharge. alienSkills.rul is parked as .ignore. The readme says it is feature complete but "has only seen testing by me", which is the honest gap. I'd put it at about 90% of what I set out to do.

Is it a real game?

Complete. It is a mod, so you need OpenXcom Extended and the original X-COM data, but on top of that it is a full campaign: story objectives, a final research chain, victory cutscenes, and the usual X-COM ways to lose. A campaign is many hours long. Expect some rough edges from limited testing.
