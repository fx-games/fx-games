Minecraft Mods

Started August 2026 · Minecraft 26.2 · Fabric · Java

⬇ DownloadSource on GitHub

This is my pile of small Fabric mods for Minecraft 26.2. Every mod lives in its own folder as a self-contained Gradle project, and one script builds and tests them all at once. Grab the jars you like from the releases page and drop them into your mods folder.

Most of them add one craftable item that changes how you get around or survive. A few are ports of older mods I missed, and some just tune the pace of the game for the way my friends and I like to play.

  • White Flag: carry it and hostile mobs leave you alone.
  • Photo Safari: photograph every creature and fill a species checklist.
  • Plush Boots, Glider, Miniature Rebreather, Fireproof Suit: no fall damage, slow steerable descents, endless air and lava immunity.
  • Climbing Claws and Grappling Hook: walls become ladders, ropes become swings.
  • Lift: a pressure plate that carries you up.
  • Seamless Crafting and Craft Tracker: crafting from nearby chests and a HUD shopping list.
  • Postbote: delivery jobs from villagers, paid in emeralds.
  • Bot Build: outline a box and a little bot builds it from your chest.
  • Stealth and Alert: mobs get real vision cones.
  • Pistol & Silencer and Immersive Aircraft (work in progress): ports of well-known mods.
  • FX Globals: longer days, slower hunger, reusable arrows, headshots and a starter kit.
