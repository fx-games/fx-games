This is my pile of small Fabric mods for Minecraft 26.2. Every mod lives in its own folder as a self-contained Gradle project, and one script builds and tests them all at once. Grab the jars you like from the releases page and drop them into your mods folder.

Most of them add one craftable item that changes how you get around or survive. A few are ports of older mods I missed, and some just tune the pace of the game for the way my friends and I like to play.

  • White Flag: carry it and hostile mobs leave you alone.
  • Photo Safari: photograph every creature and fill a species checklist.
  • Plush Boots, Glider, Miniature Rebreather, Fireproof Suit: no fall damage, slow steerable descents, endless air and lava immunity.
  • Climbing Claws and Grappling Hook: walls become ladders, ropes become swings.
  • Lift: a pressure plate that carries you up.
  • Seamless Crafting and Craft Tracker: crafting from nearby chests and a HUD shopping list.
  • Postbote: delivery jobs from villagers, paid in emeralds.
  • Bot Build: outline a box and a little bot builds it from your chest.
  • Stealth and Alert: mobs get real vision cones.
  • Pistol & Silencer and Immersive Aircraft (work in progress): ports of well-known mods.
  • FX Globals: longer days, slower hunger, reusable arrows, headshots and a starter kit.

Features

  • 17 separate Fabric mods for Minecraft 26.2, each its own Gradle project in one repo.
  • Small item mods: White Flag, Plush Boots, Glider, Miniature Rebreather, Fireproof Suit, Lift, Climbing Claws in six materials.
  • Stealth and Alert: vision cones, an awareness bar, Idle → Suspicious → Searching → Fighting, and noise that walls dampen.
  • Seamless Crafting and Craft Tracker: crafting from nearby chests and a HUD list that follows a recipe chain down to raw materials.
  • Photo Safari with Camerapture bundled inside, a species checklist and a loot mode that takes drops without killing.
  • Bot Build: a wand outlines a box, ghost blocks show the plan, a bot fetches and places the real blocks.
  • Ports of bigger mods: Grappling Hook with smithing upgrades, Pistol & Silencer, and a partial Immersive Aircraft.
  • build.sh and test.sh build every mod and run headless game tests per mod, also on GitHub Actions.

Ideas & inspirations

Several mods are ports of things I missed on this version: Stealth and Alert from RedGhostRev's NeoForge mod, the pistol from MrCrayfish's Gun Mod, Grappling Hook from Yyon's mod via weaversworkshop's Skybound fork, and Immersive Aircraft from Luke100000. Photo Safari builds on chrrs' Camerapture. The rest come from wanting one item that changes how you get around or survive, plus FX Globals to tune the pace (longer days, slower hunger, reusable arrows) for how my friends and I play. AGENTS.md sets the house rules: every mod needs an icon for Modrinth, new recipes must be unlocked from the start, and every change bumps the version.

State of the repo

Nine commits in two weeks, last one 2026-09-12. Each mod builds on its own and has game tests that CI runs. The one clearly unfinished piece is Immersive Aircraft: its README calls it a "partial port, work in progress", compile- and boot-verified but with several things only reasoned through, not flown, and integrations like Ad Astra and JEI dropped. Everything else reads as done for what it sets out to do, so I'd put the collection at about 80%.

Is it a real game?

Mods. These are add-ons for Minecraft, not a game of their own. Photo Safari's checklist and Postbote's deliveries give you goals, but the loop, the winning and the ending are still Minecraft's. You play as long as you keep playing Minecraft.
